# PRESENTATION OUTLINE: NeuroScan AI

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Brain tumors are critical medical conditions requiring early and accurate diagnosis.
- Manual inspection of MRI scans is time-consuming and prone to human error.
- Need for a fast, reliable, and interpretable AI tool for radiologists and doctors.

## Slide 3: Our Solution
- NeuroScan AI: A web-based platform for automated tumor detection.
- High-accuracy classification.
- Inclusion of Explainable AI (Grad-CAM) to provide visual justification for AI decisions.

## Slide 4: Tech Stack
- Frontend: React.js, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion (for animations).
- Backend: FastAPI, Python.
- Deep Learning: PyTorch, Torchvision (CNN Architecture).
- Deployment: Netlify (Frontend), Render/AWS (Backend).

## Slide 5: Technical Implementation (AI & ML)
- Dataset: Curated Brain MRI Dataset.
- Model Architecture: Custom CNN/Transfer Learning.
- Explainability: Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) which highlights relevant regions in the MRI scan.

## Slide 6: System Architecture
- Client (React) -> Image Upload -> API (FastAPI) -> Model Inference -> Result + Heatmap -> Client Display.
- Email Notification: Automated receipt and inquiry handling via SMTP.

## Slide 7: Live Demonstration
- UI Tour (Hero Section).
- Real-time Upload functionality.
- Analysis Result: Confidence scores and Heatmap viewing.
- Contact Form & Support System.

## Slide 8: Future Enhancements
- 3D MRI support (DICOM format).
- Multi-language support.
- Mobile application for remote diagnostics.
- Integration with Hospital Management Systems (HMS).

## Slide 9: Conclusion
- NeuroScan AI bridge the gap between AI technology and clinical diagnostics.
- Empowering medical professionals with digital assistants.
- Q&A Section.
